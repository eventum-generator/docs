Home is the screen Studio opens on. It answers two questions at a glance: what you were last working on, and whether anything is currently wrong.

**New project** scaffolds a generator project and opens it, and **Browse Hub** links to the [Eventum Hub](/hub), the catalog of ready-made generators.

## Recent projects

The generator projects whose files changed most recently, each with the number of instances running it, its size on disk and when it last changed. Clicking one opens the [project workspace](/docs/studio/projects#project-workspace).

## Instances

The instance count with a breakdown of what those instances are doing — active, inactive, and how the inactive ones ended up that way: finished, failed, or never started. A failure is visible here without opening the [Instances](/docs/studio/instances) page, and **Monitoring** links to the [dashboard](/docs/studio/monitoring) for the live figures behind the counts.

**Recent instances** lists the most recently started ones with their project, their uptime while they run, and their status.

## Explore

Three tiles for the sections that carry the day-to-day work — [Projects](/docs/studio/projects), [Instances](/docs/studio/instances) and [Scenarios](/docs/studio/scenarios).

The footer states the Eventum version, the Python version and the platform, which is the first thing worth quoting in a bug report.
