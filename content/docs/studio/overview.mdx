---

Home is the screen Studio opens on. It answers two questions at once: what you were last working on, and whether anything is currently wrong.

<Screenshot

/>

**Recent projects** holds the eight generator projects whose files changed most recently. **New project** creates one, and **Browse Hub** opens the [Eventum Hub](/hub), the catalog of ready-made generators.

The rail on the right counts every registered instance by state and lists the five most recently started with their uptime. A failure is visible here without going looking for it, and **Monitoring** leads to the [dashboard](/docs/studio/monitoring) behind the counts.

The footer states the Eventum, Python and platform versions — the first thing worth quoting in a bug report.
